## Two Engagement Models

Scalevium offers two primary tracks. Choose based on how much of the outcome you want to own.

### 1. Staff Augmentation — Talent Solutions

Vetted senior engineers join your team under your management.

- **Best for:** Teams with a technical lead (VP Eng, CTO, Engineering Manager)
- **Pricing:** Hourly or monthly retainer
- **Placement:** Within 48 hours of an approved order (often from the bench)
- **Guarantee:** Risk-free first 1–2 weeks; replacement within 3–5 business days if needed
- **Minimum engagement:** $8,000/month

### 2. Managed Pod — Technology Solutions

A complete engineering pod led by a Fractional CTO that owns delivery end to end.

- **Best for:** Non-technical founders or busy CEOs who want the outcome, not the management
- **Pricing:** Agile retainer + Fractional CTO layer (not fixed-price, fixed-scope)
- **Cadence:** Two-week sprints with weekly executive summaries and milestone demos
- **Includes:** Single point of contact, documented Definition of Ready/Done, full handover on closure

| | Staff Augmentation | Managed Pod |
|---|---|---|
| You have a technical lead | Good fit | Optional |
| You want the outcome, not the management | — | Good fit |
| Typical buyer | VP Eng, CTO, Eng Manager | Founder or CEO |
| Pricing model | Hourly / monthly retainer | Retainer + Fractional CTO |
