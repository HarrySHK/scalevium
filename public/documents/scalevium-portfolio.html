Case Study · ExperiHAUS

Hospitality & Guest Experience

ExperiHAUS

A modern hospitality platform connecting in-room guest experiences with staff operations — one system for digital guest services and property management.

The challenge

  • Properties needed a unified way to deliver digital guest services beyond a basic TV menu.
  • Guest and staff workflows had to stay separate — with clear access boundaries.
  • One backend had to power multiple client surfaces without duplicating work.

What we built

  • A shared platform backend serving guest in-room experiences and a staff admin dashboard.
  • Modular structure so property content and services can grow over time.
  • Secure session handling for guest vs. staff users on the same system.

Case Study · TowCentric

Transportation & Field Operations

TowCentric

A towing and roadside assistance platform that turns hardware alerts and field events into live dispatch work — so teams respond faster with full situational awareness.

The challenge

  • License-plate and hardware alerts needed to become actionable dispatch tickets automatically.
  • Dispatchers could not rely on manual data entry during high-volume periods.
  • Operations required live visibility — not batch updates or delayed reports.

What we built

  • Backend ingestion that converts hardware webhook events into structured dispatch tickets.
  • Real-time dashboards powered by WebSocket updates for dispatch teams.
  • Workflow-oriented UI so operators track ticket state from alert to resolution.

Our role

Backend development Real-time dashboards Webhook integration Frontend development
